Guess My Job

Listen to the schedule clues. Guess the job. The fewer clues you need, the more points you win.

  • 1. Show one clue. Teams raise a hand to guess.
  • 2. Wrong guess? Show the next clue. Each new clue is worth 1 point less.
  • 3. Right guess? Show the answer and give the points to that team.
