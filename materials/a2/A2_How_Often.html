How Often?

How to use

Read the question. Move the slider to answer. The answer sentence changes too.

Word order

Adverbs go before the main verb: I usually work late.
But after am / is / are: I am never late.
Expressions go at the end: I work late once a week.

Keys

← → move · N next question · H hide the word · M switch mode
